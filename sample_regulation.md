# Article 1 Scope

This Regulation applies to controllers and processors that process personal data in the context of providing digital services within the United Kingdom.

# Article 2 Security Obligation

Controllers must implement appropriate technical and organisational controls to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

# Article 3 Exception

The security obligation does not apply where the data has been irreversibly anonymized.

# Article 4 Citation

This Regulation should be read together with Article 32 GDPR where relevant.
